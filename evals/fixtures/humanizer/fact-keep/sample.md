**Overview:** Finance Ops' new invoice-matching tool stands as a testament to what thoughtful automation can achieve — not just a faster month-end, but a transformed one. Since its March 4 rollout, it has quietly matched about 82% of supplier invoices on its own, a striking leap from the 61% the old spreadsheet process managed.

- **Range:** invoices from €50 to €20,000; anything larger still lands with Dana Reyes for manual review.
- **Audience:** designed with the accounts-payable clerks in mind — not the auditors.
- **Scope:** only PDF invoices, and only from the Lisbon and Porto offices.

Every night at 02:00 the matching runs, and by the next morning any errors are waiting in the AP lead's inbox, ensuring nothing slips through the cracks. In short, it is seamless, robust, and future-proof — a tool that truly empowers the team to focus on what matters most.
